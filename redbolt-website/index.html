FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a website cost?

Every project is scoped individually. After a free consultation we send a fixed quote with clear deliverables and timelines — no surprises.

How long does a project take?

A marketing website typically takes 3–6 weeks. Web apps and e-commerce stores depend on scope; we'll share a milestone plan up front.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. We audit your current site, keep what works, migrate your content and preserve your SEO rankings during the move.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes — our care plans cover hosting, updates, backups, security monitoring and small content changes every month.
