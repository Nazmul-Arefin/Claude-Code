(06) — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Ask us anything.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is scoped individually. After a free consultation we send a fixed quote with clear deliverables and timelines — no surprises.

Do you build custom software?

Yes. We build inventory, school management, point-of-sale and HR systems — and we run our own SaaS products, Amar Finance, Hello Campus and Bolt Mail.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. We audit your current site, keep what works, migrate your content and protect your SEO rankings during the move.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes — our tech support covers hosting, updates, backups, security monitoring and content changes.

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?

We do. We work remotely with clients anywhere, with calls scheduled in your time zone.
